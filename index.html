Who We Are

Where faith meets creativity.

Zimrah Arts Network is an evangelical ministry that believes the Gospel can be communicated through more than words alone.

Through music, film, drama, painting, writing, photography and other creative expressions, we seek to proclaim Jesus Christ, inspire faith and touch lives in ways that connect with the heart.

Our desire is to see artists discover that their creativity is not merely a talent, but an opportunity to serve God, influence society and become a voice for the Gospel.

The Name

What does Zimrah mean?

"Zimrah" is a Hebrew word meaning song, melody or praise. It captures our heartbeat: creativity offered to God as worship, and used to carry the Gospel to the nations.

Our Conviction

Why Creative Arts?

Stories move people. Music carries messages. Film opens conversations. Drama makes ideas visible. Painting speaks without words. Writing gives language to experiences. Photography preserves moments. Creativity can open doors for the Gospel.

We believe that when artistic gifts are surrendered to God, creativity can become a powerful expression of faith, truth, hope and love.

Your gift can become a voice for the Gospel.
